Book an EEG session

What to expect

I fit a headphone with sensors in the earmuffs on your head, set up the equipment, and record while you do some simple tasks. Nothing is painful or invasive.

90 minutes for one person, setup included. Each additional person in your household adds 60 minutes, since the equipment is already set up. For example, 3 people take 3.5 hours. Book as many people as fits your day.
